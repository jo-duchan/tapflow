Flow Capture's Match ends with a review when the app did not respond to some of the recorded iOS taps (`acted: false`): one line says how many will be left out, Continue alone leaves them out, and the line opens onto a checklist, each row showing the screen it was tapped on, to keep any. A tap Match saw change the screen, or one right before typing, is never offered. Left-out taps show in Edit as not in the flow, with a button that puts one back, and are not replayed by Verify or exported. flow-runner adds `noResponseTaps`, `leaveOut` and the `no-response` skip reason. A tap made again right after on the same named element, at least a second apart and not a double tap or a field, the recorded screen not a cell different between the two (upright only), is dropped before the replay (`DropReason` `'retried'`; flow-runner adds `retryCandidates` and `dropRetried`, the dashboard confirms by the recorded pictures). A dropped tap says "Not replayed" inside its Range tile. The dashboard's disclosures (App Center releases, trace steps, Edit's step details, the upload's Override version) open and close with motion: they are shadcn's Accordion and Collapsible now, on Radix. When the step after a tap looks different and that tap left the replay's screen unchanged where the recording's changed, Match replays the tap once more before stopping (`MatchHooks.tapDidNothing`); Verify and flow run are unchanged.
