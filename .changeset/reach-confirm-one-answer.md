A touch on an element reached at its centre, in the frame the screen was just read in, is pressed on the first reach answer instead of after a second tree read and reach: on a busy iOS app each took about 2 s, and the step ran out of time confirming a point that never moved. A step that does run out says the device answered too slowly, with how long a reach and a read of the screen took; "kept moving" is said only when two answers disagreed. The relay ships the dashboard, whose Flow Capture Verify runs this engine.
