Flow Capture's Match looks again, for up to 3 seconds, at a screen that does not look like the recording's before it stops there: a screen still on its way (a login's answer drawn after its spinner, a splash before the first screen) now goes on once it matches. When it came late, the screen is read again before the step, so a tap the app named is replayed on its element there rather than at its point.
