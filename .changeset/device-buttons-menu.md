The device toolbar's Device group puts the hardware buttons behind one "⋯" menu (**More device buttons**) on both platforms. iOS gains volume up and down, Sleep/Wake and, on devices that have it, Action, which were reachable only on the device frame. Android's volume and power buttons move from the toolbar into the same menu. Volume keeps the menu open so it can be pressed again; an iPad's volume is named by what it does once turned, as on the frame.
